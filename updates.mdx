<Update label="November 2025" tags={["Feature"]}>
  Added ability to Append on Scan.  If a recipient scans a mailer and we don’t have their email or phone, we’ll research and add it. Each lookup costs 20¢, and some records may require two lookups for best accuracy.

  To Toggle this feature on/of visit your [Mailer Settings](https://dashboard.thanks.io/profile/settings).
</Update>
