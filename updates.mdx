## Auto-Recharge Disable Toggle

You now have full control over auto-recharge directly from **Billing settings**.

Turn it off when you want tighter spend control, then turn it back on anytime with your preferred settings ready to go.

### What's new

- A clear on/off toggle for auto-recharge
- Prominent Billing warnings before and after disabling
- Your saved recharge amount is kept for when you turn it back on

### How it works

1. Auto-recharge on: sending continues as usual, with automatic charging if your balance would go below $0.
2. Auto-recharge off: sends that would push your balance below $0 are paused.
3. You’ll see a clear message and can add credits manually or re-enable auto-recharge.

### Important impact

- Outbound mail can pause if your balance is too low
- Keep a positive balance or top up manually to avoid delivery interruptions
- Manual **Add Credits** always works

### Heads up

Disabling auto-recharge means mailers can stop once your balance goes below $0. Keep credits available or add funds manually to avoid delivery interruptions.


</Update>


<Update label="June 5" tags={["New Feature"]} >

## Dynamic Image Fields Now Support Custom Field Image URLs

You can now use recipient custom field tokens in dynamic image URL fields, including **Background Image**, **Logo Image URL**, and **Overlay Graphic**.

Supported tokens:

- `~CUSTOM1~` through `~CUSTOM10~`

At send time, each token resolves to the recipient's custom field value, allowing that value to point to a PNG or JPG URL and render in the final generated image.

### What this enables

- Recipient-specific background images
- Recipient-specific logos or brand images
- Recipient-specific overlay graphics
- Flexible templates that source image assets from recipient data instead of hardcoded URLs

### How it works

- The dynamic image editor recognizes custom field tokens in image URL fields
- Preview rendering shows placeholder images when a custom field token is used as an image source
- At send time, the system resolves the recipient's custom field value into the final image URL
- If the custom field is missing or not a valid image URL, that recipient is skipped and the order is flagged with an error instead of printing a broken card

### Important behavior

- If a custom field value does not include `http://` or `https://`, the system automatically prepends `https://`
- This applies to dynamic image fields only, not ordinary text personalization

### Why this matters

- More personalized creative
- Fewer manual template variations
- Safer production handling when image data is missing or invalid

</Update>

## Custom Fields Expanded to custom1–custom10

Recipients now support ten custom fields — `custom1` through `custom10` — up from the previous limit of four. The additional fields are available everywhere custom fields are supported:

- **Mailing Lists** — add and manage all ten fields per recipient
- **API** — pass `custom5` through `custom10` in recipient objects on any send-mailer endpoint
- **Zapier** — map up to ten custom fields when creating or updating recipients

All ten fields can be used as merge tags in **message templates** and as dynamic variables in **image templates**, just like the original four.

</Update>

## Clone Campaign Automations

You can now clone any campaign automation directly from the campaign list. Instead of rebuilding the same trigger logic, schedule, and mailer settings from scratch, duplicate an existing automation and adjust only what you need.

This is useful when running similar campaigns for different mailing lists, seasonal variations of a proven automation, or setting up A/B structures across separate campaigns.

### How it works

1. Open the **Campaigns** page
2. Find the automation you want to duplicate
3. Click the **Clone** option from the campaign actions menu
4. The cloned automation opens ready to edit — rename it, swap the mailing list, or adjust the schedule

All trigger settings, mailer selections, and timing rules are copied over. The clone starts in a paused state so you can review it before activating.

## ThanksAI Studio is here

We've shipped something we're genuinely excited about. **ThanksAI Studio** is an AI-powered creative workspace built directly into thanks.io that turns a plain-English description into a print-ready direct mail design - in under a minute.

Describe what you want. Paste your website. Hit send.

No design software. No creative agency. No waiting.

### What you can create

ThanksAI Studio supports all 7 of our mailer formats:

| Format | Size |
|--------|------|
| Postcard | 4×6 and 6×9 |
| Jumbo Postcard | 6×11 |
| Windowed Letter | 8.5×11" |
| Windowless Letter | 8.5×11" |
| Notecard / Greeting Card | 5.625×4.3125" |
| Gift Card | 5.625×4.3125" |

### Dynamic QR Codes - built in, not bolted on

<Tip>
Every design generated by ThanksAI Studio includes a **live, trackable QR code** composited directly into the artwork at print resolution.
</Tip>

Here's what makes them special:

- **Any destination URL.** Point the QR code anywhere — your homepage, a landing page, a property listing, a booking link. You control it.
- **Merge tag support.** The QR destination is personalized per recipient at send time. Include `~FIRST_NAME~`, address fields (`~ADDRESS~`), or any custom attribute(`~CUSTOM1~`) — each recipient gets a QR code that links to *their* unique URL.
- **Click tracking included.** Every scan is routed through thanks.io's tracking layer, so you see exactly who scanned and when.
- **Flexible placement.** Ask for the QR in the top-right, bottom-center, or anywhere else in natural language. The AI positions it precisely on the canvas.
- **Optional.** Don't want a QR code? Just say so and the AI removes it entirely.

### Intelligent brand detection

Paste any URL and ThanksAI Studio automatically extracts your brand identity - or your client's:

- **Logo and accent color** pulled directly from the domain
- **Company name, phone, email, and disclaimer** resolved automatically
- Works with listing pages on Zillow, Redfin, Yelp, and other aggregator sites — the AI extracts the *business being promoted*, not the platform
- **Account Branding** overrides everything: store your name, logo, colors, and contact info once and they apply to every design you create

### Iterate with chat

Your design isn't locked after the first generation. ThanksAI Studio includes a full conversational workspace:

- **Describe changes in plain English** — "Make the background warmer," "Move the QR code to the bottom-left," "Add our phone number in white text"
- **Attach reference images** — upload a photo, logo, or inspiration image directly in the chat
- **The AI remembers context** across messages, so you don't repeat yourself
- Every iteration builds on the previous design, changing only what you asked

### From concept to campaign in 4 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your brief">
    Describe the mailer in plain English and (optionally) paste your website URL. The AI detects the right format automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your design">
    Your AI-generated design appears in seconds. Chat to refine it as many times as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the checklist">
    Before saving, confirm the design looks right: no AI artifacts, accurate text, QR code properly placed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and send">
    Save as a reusable template and jump directly into Card Builder to start your campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ThanksAI Studio is live for all thanks.io accounts. Find it under **ThanksAI Studio** in the main navigation, or [Click Here](https://dashboard.thanks.io/creative-generations) to get started.

<Update label="April 25" tags={["Improvement"]}>
  ## Add Branding Info as Message Template Replacement Variables

  - Added support for using branding information as replacement variables in Message Templates. This allows you to easily include your company name, phone, and other branding elements in your mailers without having to hardcode them into each template.
  - The following replacement variables are now available for use in Message Templates:
    - %YOUR_FULL_NAME% - Your Full Name
    - %YOUR_FIRST_NAME% - Your First Name
    - %YOUR_PHONE% - Your Phone
    - %YOUR_COMPANY% - Your Company
    - %YOUR_EMAIL% - Your Email Address
</Update>

<Update label="August 21" tags={["New Feature"]}>
  ## AI Fonts

  - Added support for AI-generated fonts for your mailers.
  - These fonts totally randomize character placement and spacing to create a unique look for each mailer. This can help increase engagement and make your mailers stand out in the mailbox.
  - Realism mode will automatically add crossouts and other imperfections to make the font look more like it was handwritten by a human.
  - To use AI fonts, simply select the 'AI Font' option when creating or editing a Message Template. You can also toggle the 'Realism Mode' option to add imperfections to the font
</Update>
