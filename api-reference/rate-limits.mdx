---
title: Rate Limits
description: "Understand the rate limits for the thanks.io API."
mode: "wide"
---

We limit API calls to 60 requests per minute. If you exceed this limit, you'll receive a `429 Too Many Requests` response. 

If your use case requires a higher rate limit, please contact our support team at [support@thanks.io](mailto:support@thanks.io).

The [thanks.io MCP server](/thanks-io-mcp-server#limits) for AI assistants has its own limits: 120 requests per minute per account, and at most 10 orders per minute per account.
