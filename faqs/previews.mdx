---
title: 'Order Preview'
description: 'How do I preview an order?'
---

Any order can be previewed by setting the `preview` parameter to true during a [Send Mailer](/api-reference/send-mailer-usage) request - links to PNG previews of the mailer will be returned in the response.

On the Free and Business plans, preview images carry a **DRAFT** watermark. The mail you send prints without it. Previews on the Professional and Enterprise plans have no watermark.
