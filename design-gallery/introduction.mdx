## Templates

Start with these templates to build your designs. Open them in Canva or your preferred design tool, and don’t forget to delete the sample graphics.

## Inspirations

Use these ideas however you like-customize them with your branding or use them right out of the box. When you're finished, upload your designs to your [thanks.io Image Templates](https://dashboard.thanks.io/image_templates). Each upload appears in your Creatives library; pair it with a message there and you can send the finished creative through the API with a single `creative_id`. See [Send a Creative](/api-reference/send-mailer-usage#send-a-creative).
