# Print Zones

Measured in pixels on the upload-size image above. Each size is measured in from the edge or corner named.

| Zone | Size | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleed** | Outer 23px on every edge | Trimmed off. Extend background colors and images into it, but keep nothing important there. |
| **Safe zone** | 48px in from every edge | Keep all text, logos, and QR codes inside this line. Anything between it and the bleed may be cut off. |
| **Magnet (front)** | 1145px wide × 783px tall, from the top-left corner | The magnet is cut from this area. Keep the magnet's content inside it. |
| **Back right column** | 1188px wide, from the right edge, full height | Keep it clear. The magnet attaches in the top part and the address and postage print on a white block in the bottom part (864px tall, from the bottom-right corner). |
