# Print Zones

Measured in pixels on the upload-size image above. Each size is measured in from the edge or corner named.

| Zone | Size | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleed** | Outer 18px | Trimmed off. On the outer front template this is the left, right, and bottom edges only. |
| **Safe zone** | 44px in from every edge | Keep all text, logos, and QR codes inside this line. Anything between it and the bleed may be cut off. |

Notecards mail in an envelope, so there is no address block on the card.
