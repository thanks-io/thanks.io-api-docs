# Upload Size

| | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixels** | 1875px | 1275px |
| **Inches** | 6.25in | 4.25in |

# Print Zones

Measured in pixels on the upload-size image above. Each size is measured in from the edge or corner named.

| Zone | Size | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleed** | Outer 37px on every edge | Trimmed off. Extend background colors and images into it, but keep nothing important there. |
| **Safe zone** | 60px in from every edge | Keep all text, logos, and QR codes inside this line. Anything between it and the bleed may be cut off. |
| **Address block** | 1067px wide × 780px tall, from the bottom-right corner | The recipient address and postage print on a white block here, covering your design. |
| **QR code area** | 440px wide × 455px tall, from the top-right corner | Leave empty if you add a thanks.io QR code to your order. |
