# Upload Size

| | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixels** | 2738px | 1838px |
| **Inches** | 9.125in | 6.125in |

This is exactly what prints: a 9in × 6in card with a 0.0625in bleed on every side.

# Print Zones

Measured in pixels on the upload-size image above. Each size is measured in from the edge or corner named.

| Zone | Size | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleed** | Outer 19px on every edge | Trimmed off. Extend background colors and images into it, but keep nothing important there. |
| **Safe zone** | 42px in from every edge | Keep all text, logos, and QR codes inside this line. Anything between it and the bleed may be cut off. |
| **Address block** | 1193px wide × 923px tall, from the bottom-right corner | The recipient address and postage print on a white block here, covering your design. |
