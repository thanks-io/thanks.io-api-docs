# Address Area

thanks.io prints each recipient's name and address in the upper-left area of page 1, where it shows through the envelope window. Keep that area blank in your design. This includes PDF Only letters sent with `pdf_only_url`: do not put a recipient address in your PDF, or it will be printed over.
